Insights + Answers

Two supporting formats for a future employment site.

Insights and Answers can create organized places for useful search content without crowding the future sister site's service, attorney, or firm pages.

Insights

The normal editorial blog.

Full articles for topics that deserve context, analysis, examples, and a practical point of view. These are authored, attorney-reviewed, and organized by useful themes.

Answers

One question, answered quickly.

Short, direct responses to narrow questions. Each Answer identifies its supporting source and can point readers to a relevant Insight, service, or attorney when more depth is useful.

What stays simple

The future sister site remains focused.

Service and firm pages stay centered on clarity, confidence, and a simple path to contact. Search content lives in these two supporting areas and connects selectively, without keyword stuffing or turning every page into an article.

90-day roadmap

Finish the plan, approve the build, then expand what works.

The roadmap separates work we can safely do now from paid production and post-launch learning.

01

Planning now

Align the audience and site model.

  • Use the current feedback to draft the audience map, service groups, and first-release sitemap.
  • Prioritize executives, physicians, and employer counsel while broader individual matters remain conditional.
  • Refresh only the keyword, competitor, and local-search evidence needed to confirm page order.
  • Define conflict-first intake, attorney review, ownership, and measurement requirements.
02

After proposal acceptance

Design and build the smallest coherent site.

  • Confirm the sister-site name, domain, brand relationship, and final architecture.
  • Design and develop the approved first-release page set.
  • Prepare original legal content for named attorney reviewers.
  • Test accessibility, privacy-safe inquiry tracking, technical SEO, and conflict routing before launch.
03

After an approved launch

Learn before expanding.

  • Review which pages Google has added, which searches reach them, what visitors do next, and which inquiries fit.
  • Improve approved public profiles and begin selective authority building.
  • Compare search visibility and qualified inquiries across the main site and sister site.
  • Expand the topics and campaigns that show the strongest fit.
