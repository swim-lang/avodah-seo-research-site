How to read the reports

Six terms make the data easier to use.

Every number is labeled as evidence, an estimate, a recommendation, or an unresolved decision. These definitions explain the most common measurements.

Search volume

An estimate of how often a phrase is searched. It is demand evidence, not promised traffic.

Visibility

How often tracked keywords place a site where searchers can realistically see it.

Click share

The portion of available searches that could become visits at a given level of ranking visibility.

Conversion

The portion of visits that become inquiries. A qualified matter still depends on fit, intake, and conflicts.

Scenario

Transparent math used to understand a range of outcomes. It is not a forecast or guarantee.

SERP

The search-results page, including maps, ads, directories, questions, AI features, and normal listings.
